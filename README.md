# Smart Shop Management System

A professional, modern, and high-performance Shop Management Web Application designed for small to medium-sized businesses. This system provides a seamless experience for managing inventory, tracking sales, monitoring expenses, and gaining deep business insights through Integrated Artificial Intelligence.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Full Persian (RTL) Support**: Tailored for the Persian language and Jalali context.
- **Dynamic Dashboard**: Real-time analytics and visual data representation.
- **Inventory Management**: Comprehensive product tracking with low-stock alerts.
- **Sales & Revenue Tracking**: Detailed transaction history with profit calculation.
- **Expense Control**: Monitor operational costs to calculate true net profit.
- **AI-Powered Insights**: Uses Google Gemini 3 Flash to analyze your data and provide business growth tips.
- **Offline-First**: Uses local storage to ensure your data is persistent within your browser.

## 📸 Section Breakdown

### 1. Interactive Dashboard
The central hub of the application. It provides an immediate overview of total sales, net profit, total expenses, and inventory warnings. The integrated Area Chart allows you to visualize sales trends over time.

![Dashboard Section Placeholder](https://via.placeholder.com/800x450?text=Dashboard+Screenshot)
*Key functionality: Quick stats, Sales graph, and Low-stock alerts.*

### 2. Inventory & Product Management
A robust system to add, edit, and track your products. You can manage sizes, colors, purchase prices, and selling prices while setting custom reorder points for automatic stock warnings.

![Inventory Section Placeholder](https://via.placeholder.com/800x450?text=Inventory+Management+Screenshot)
*Key functionality: Product search, Stock tracking, and Price management.*

### 3. Sales Management
Effortlessly register new sales. The system automatically fetches prices, calculates totals and profits, and updates the inventory stock in real-time. It also maintains a searchable history of all past transactions.

![Sales Section Placeholder](https://via.placeholder.com/800x450?text=Sales+Management+Screenshot)
*Key functionality: Invoice creation, Automated stock deduction, and Sales history.*

### 4. Expense Tracking
Keep a close eye on your business costs (Rent, Bills, Salaries, etc.). By tracking expenses, the system provides a more accurate Net Profit calculation on the main dashboard.

![Expenses Section Placeholder](https://via.placeholder.com/800x450?text=Expense+Tracking+Screenshot)
*Key functionality: Cost logging, Category descriptions, and Expense list.*

### 5. AI Business Insights
Leveraging the power of **Google Gemini AI**, this section analyzes your current inventory and sales data to give you professional advice on how to optimize your stock and increase your revenue.

![AI Insights Placeholder](https://via.placeholder.com/800x450?text=AI+Insights+Screenshot)
*Key functionality: Intelligent data analysis, Growth suggestions, and Performance reports.*

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React 19 (TypeScript)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Charts**: Recharts
- **AI Engine**: Google GenAI (Gemini 3 Flash)
- **Data Persistence**: LocalStorage API

## ⚙️ Installation & Setup

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/your-username/smart-shop-manager.git
   ```
2. **Open the project**:
   Simply open the `index.html` file in any modern web browser.
3. **AI Configuration**:
   Ensure you have a valid Google Gemini API Key. The application uses `process.env.API_KEY` to connect to the AI services.
