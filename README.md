# Smart Shop Management System

A professional, full-featured Shop Management Web Application built for modern businesses. This system provides a seamless, high-performance experience for inventory tracking, sales processing, expense monitoring, and data-driven decision-making powered by **Google Gemini AI**.

---

## 🚀 Key Highlights

- **🌍 Fully Localized**: Native RTL (Right-to-Left) support with Persian (Farsi) language integration.
- **📊 Real-time Analytics**: Dynamic dashboard with interactive charts and live business metrics.
- **📦 Intelligent Inventory**: Automated stock tracking with custom reorder point alerts.
- **🤖 AI-Powered Strategy**: Integrated Gemini 3 Flash to analyze business data and provide growth insights.
- **🛡️ Secure & Private**: Offline-first architecture using LocalStorage to keep your data on your own device.
- **🎨 Modern UI/UX**: Clean, responsive, and aesthetic design built with Tailwind CSS.

---

## 🛠 Feature Breakdown

### 📊 1. Professional Dashboard
The nerve center of your business. It offers a bird's-eye view of your financial health.
- **Metrics**: Track Total Sales, Net Profit, and Operational Expenses at a glance.
- **Visuals**: Interactive Area Charts powered by *Recharts* to visualize revenue trends.
- **Alerts**: Real-time notifications for low-stock items that need immediate attention.

### 📦 2. Inventory & Stock Control
A comprehensive management tool for your product catalog.
- **Details**: Manage products by name, size, color, and SKU.
- **Pricing**: Track both purchase and selling prices to calculate margins automatically.
- **Smart Warnings**: Highlighted stock levels that change color when hitting reorder points.

### 💰 3. Sales & Invoice Processing
Streamlined transaction management to keep your business moving.
- **Automation**: Selecting a product automatically calculates totals and potential profit.
- **Stock Sync**: Inventory levels are updated instantly upon every sale.
- **History**: A detailed, searchable log of all past transactions with easy cancellation options.

### 💳 4. Expense Management
Maintain a clear picture of your cash flow by tracking operational costs.
- **Categorization**: Log rent, bills, salaries, and maintenance.
- **Profit Impact**: Expenses are subtracted from total gross profit to show your true bottom line.

### 🤖 5. AI Business Insights (Gemini AI)
Take the guesswork out of business growth with cutting-edge AI.
- **Analysis**: The system sends an anonymized summary of your data to Google Gemini.
- **Outcomes**: Receive professional advice on inventory optimization, high-performing products, and cost-saving tips.
- **Language**: All AI outputs are delivered in Persian for maximum utility.

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **Frontend Framework**: [React 19](https://react.dev/) (TypeScript)
- **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **Iconography**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Charts**: [Recharts](https://recharts.org/)
- **AI Integration**: [Google GenAI SDK](https://ai.google.dev/) (Gemini 3 Flash)
- **Data Layer**: Browser LocalStorage API

## ⚙️ Installation & Setup

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/your-username/smart-shop-manager.git
   ```
2. **Launch the app**:
   This project is a standalone web application. Simply open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).
3. **Configure AI**:
   To use the AI Insights feature, ensure your environment provides a valid Google Gemini API Key.
